## 5. 커버리지 측정 (gcov / lcov)

### 5.1 빌드 설정

`CMakeLists.txt`에 `ENABLE_COVERAGE` 옵션 **미구현**. `docs/test_plan.md` §7.1 MinGW 플래그를 `build_cov/`에 적용.

```powershell
# 주의: -DENABLE_COVERAGE=ON + MSVC는 u8"..." 컴파일 오류 → MinGW 필수
Remove-Item -Recurse -Force build_cov -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
cmake -S c:\DEV\FeedbackAnalyzer_12 -B c:\DEV\FeedbackAnalyzer_12\build_cov `
  -G "MinGW Makefiles" `
  -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=C:/mingw64/bin/g++.exe `
  -DCMAKE_CXX_FLAGS="--coverage -O0 -g" `
  -DCMAKE_EXE_LINKER_FLAGS="--coverage"
cmake --build c:\DEV\FeedbackAnalyzer_12\build_cov
ctest --test-dir c:\DEV\FeedbackAnalyzer_12\build_cov --output-on-failure

lcov --capture --directory build_cov --output-file coverage.info
lcov --remove coverage.info "*/tests/*" "*/googletest/*" "*/httplib.h" --output-file coverage.filtered.info
genhtml coverage.filtered.info --output-directory build_cov/coverage_html
```

- **도구:** gcov 15.2.0 (MinGW), lcov, gcovr 8.6
